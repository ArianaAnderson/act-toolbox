Voice-based assessment

Assessment that meets participants where they are.

Standardized tasks delivered remotely, with voice responses captured for automated transcription and scoring.

FRONT-END PROTOTYPE

A clearer view of cognition and affect.

PTSD can affect memory, attention, executive function, and everyday life. ACT is being developed to make repeated measurement easier—reducing travel and administrative burden while expanding access to research.

01 / SYMPTOMS

Self-report measures

Track PTSD symptoms alongside anxiety and depression using established research instruments.

02 / COGNITION

Verbal cognitive tests

Assess fluency, working memory, episodic memory, executive function, and attention remotely.

03 / AFFECT

Vocal-affect measures

Study acoustic features of speech as complementary biological signals of participants’ affective states.

First assessment module

Animal Naming Test

Participants name as many animals as possible in one minute. The planned module will securely record a response, transcribe it, identify valid unique animal names, and return a transparent score for researcher review.
